Le Roy General Rules

Black Dice Combat: Le Roy uses his strongest available die to eliminate ONLY the highest remaining black die. Any remaining black dice pass to the player.
Purchasing Dice: When purchasing Le Roy's dice on your turn, pay the appropriate deniers to the bank.
Negative Events: Negative events do NOT affect Le Roy. Any event targeting the neutral player targets Le Roy instead (meeples & cubes).

Card Modifications Reference

Tax Collector: Gain 2 denier per citizen in City Hall (instead of 1).
Tithing: Take up to 3 of Le Roy's yellow dice (max 3/action).
Migrant Workers: If cube placed in Cathedral, Le Roy +1 VP.

Final Scoring Rules

• Receive points for each remaining character card.
• Final Score: Subtract Le Roy's VP from your total score.

Dice & Placement Priority Rules

Priority Order: Higher Value > Red > Yellow > White.

Building Target by Color:

  • Red: Palace
  • Yellow: City Hall
  • White: Bishopric

Le Roy places on the best available spots in each building. Equal numbers are resolved by color order. Dice values are irrelevant for exact building placement once priority order determines which 3 dice are used.
